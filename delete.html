Account Deletion Request

Delete your account and associated data

To delete your Yeriho account, you must submit the official deletion request form. Please read the warning and steps below before continuing.

Irreversible action Form submission required Processed within 7–30 days (after verification)

Important warning

Account deletion is a permanent request. After deletion, you may lose access to your saved content and app features. Submit the form only if you are sure.

Please read before submitting

Use the same email address that you used in the Yeriho app. Incorrect details may delay verification. Deletion requests cannot be undone.

How to fill the deletion request form

When the form opens, fill the following fields carefully:

  • Email Address * — Enter the same email you used in the Yeriho app.
  • Reason for Deletion * — This is required. Please write a short reason.
  • Confirmation * — Tick the checkbox confirming you want deletion.
  • Submit — Tap the Submit button to send your request.
Use correct email

Use the same email you used in the app. This helps us locate your account quickly.

Reason is required

Please provide a clear reason (example: “No longer using the app”). The form requires this field.

Do not share passwords

Never enter your password in the form or email. The request should not require passwords.

Timeline

After submission and verification, deletion is processed within 7–30 days.

Support

For faster and easier replies, please contact us from the app using In-App Support. Email support is also available.
