Request Account Deletion

Use this page to request deletion of your Yeriho account. You must submit the deletion request form. After your request is verified, deletion is processed within 7–30 days.

Form required
Confirmation email after deletion
Form can be submitted only once
Apply for Deletion Contact Support
Important: The deletion request form can be submitted only one time. If you submitted it by mistake, please contact support using the email mentioned in the Support section.

What happens after you submit?

  • 1
    Fill the deletion request form

    Enter the correct details so we can locate and verify your account.

  • 2
    We verify the request

    We may verify that the request belongs to the account owner.

  • 3
    Deletion is processed (7–30 days)

    Once verified, the account deletion is completed within the stated timeline.

  • 4
    Confirmation email

    After deletion is completed, you will receive a confirmation email.

If you do not see the form button working, it means the form link is not added yet. We will update the button link later.

Instructions to fill the request form

Use the same account details

Provide the same details you used in the app (email/phone/user id, if asked). Wrong details may delay verification.

Submit only once

The form can be submitted only one time. Please double-check before submitting. If you submitted by mistake, contact support.

Timeline

Account deletion typically takes 7–30 days after verification. You’ll receive a confirmation email once completed.

Need help?

If you face issues, use the Support section on the Home page. Keep your request details ready while contacting support.

Tip: After we add the Google Form link, the Apply for Deletion button will open the form in a new tab.
