Privacy Policy

This page explains how Yeriho collects, uses, and protects information. For questions, contact support@yeriho.com.

🔒
Quick summary Yeriho may collect basic account/support information, app diagnostics, and content you choose to create or upload. We use it to provide the app, improve reliability, and respond to support requests. You can request account deletion anytime.

1) Information we collect

Depending on how you use Yeriho, we may collect:

  • Account information (e.g., email/phone if you sign in, and identifiers needed to operate the app).
  • Support information (messages, attachments, and contact details you send to support).
  • User content you create or upload in the app (for example notes, images, files, or other content depending on features you use).
  • Device & diagnostics (crash reports, performance data, device model/OS version, app version, and similar technical data).
  • Usage data (feature interactions and basic analytics, if enabled in the app build).

We do not sell your personal information.

2) How we use information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the app and its features.
  • Authenticate users (if sign-in is used).
  • Store and sync user content (when cloud features are used).
  • Detect, prevent, and fix bugs, crashes, and security issues.
  • Respond to support requests and communicate important updates.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

3) Sharing

We may share information only in these cases:

  • Service providers (e.g., hosting, analytics, crash reporting, email/support tooling) who process data for us.
  • Legal reasons if required by law or to protect rights and safety.
  • Business transfers if the app is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale (you’ll be notified if required).

We do not share your personal information for advertising sale purposes.

4) Storage & security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Your device may also store some data locally (e.g., cached content) depending on app features and settings.

5) Retention

We retain information only as long as needed to provide the app, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

You can request deletion using the account deletion page, or by emailing support@yeriho.com.

✅
Support note Email is the official support channel. If in-app support is available in your app version, replies there may be faster and easier.

6) Your choices & rights

You may have rights depending on your location, including:

  • Request access to or correction of your information.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Opt out of certain analytics (if the app provides such controls).

To request account deletion, use Account deletion.

7) Children

Yeriho is not intended for children under the age required by local law for consent. If you believe a child provided personal information, contact support to request deletion.

8) Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Effective date” above will be updated. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

9) Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or data handling, contact:

Email: support@yeriho.com
